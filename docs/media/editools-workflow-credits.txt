EDITools public-data workflow demonstration (2x playback)

Magnetotelluric observations:
Peacock, Jared, and Erika Gasperikova (2020).
Magnetotelluric Data from Mountain Home, ID.
United States Geological Survey / Geothermal Data Repository.
https://doi.org/10.15121/1666377
https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1249
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The demonstration uses a geographic subset of the published EDI archive.
Station labels are shortened for readability. The optional NFREQ attribute
on >FREQ headers is omitted in working copies for importer compatibility;
the frequency count after // and all numeric measurements are retained. EDITools derives the plotted
quantities from the published measurements. Mask edits are demonstrated
and undone in memory; the downloaded EDI measurements are unchanged.

Geographic backgrounds:
OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
Natural Earth, public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
Topography: USGS 3DEP, public domain, downloaded through EDITools.
https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program

The animation records the actual application interface. Pointer movements, highlighted period bands
and explanatory captions are added by the recorder. Playback runs at twice
the scripted interaction speed; network waiting time is condensed.
No endorsement by the data providers is implied.

This version follows the southwest–northeast branch from MHW5 to MH07,
with a 0.5 km corridor half-width. The profile includes MHW5, MHW4, MHW3,
MHW2, MHW1, MH19, MH18, MH17, MH01, MH05, MH06 and MH07.
It shows resistivity/phase pseudosections, profile phase-tensor ellipses,
and PT-azimuth and real/imaginary tipper-normal rose diagrams.
The existing map-layer and actual topography-download footage is retained.
